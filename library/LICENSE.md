The symbols, footprints and parts database in `library/` are licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-SA 4.0)**, with the same exception KiCad's own libraries
grant: **using these symbols and footprints in your designs places no licence obligation on the designs or the
files you produce from them** (schematics, PCBs, Gerbers, BoMs). The exception covers designs only; if you
redistribute the library itself, modified or not, it stays CC-BY-SA 4.0.

Several footprints are derived from KiCad's official libraries (CC-BY-SA 4.0 with that exception).
Manufacturer names and part numbers are used only to identify parts. Datasheets and 3D models are not included:
fetch them from the manufacturer. See `EXCLUDED.md` for what the maintainers' library holds but cannot
redistribute.

Footprints are drawn from the manufacturers' recommended land patterns but **you must verify every footprint
against the current datasheet before ordering boards** (the pe-schematic skill makes this a required step).
